# How Social Media Advertising Works

## Overview

Social media advertising uses sophisticated targeting and algorithms to deliver relevant ads to users.

## Ad Platforms

### Major Ad Networks
- **Meta Ads**: Facebook and Instagram advertising
- **Google Ads**: Search and display advertising
- **LinkedIn Ads**: Professional audience targeting
- **TikTok Ads**: Short-form video advertising

## Ad Targeting

### Audience Segmentation
- **Demographic Targeting**: Age, gender, location
- **Interest Targeting**: Targeting based on user interests
- **Behavioral Targeting**: Based on online behavior
- **Lookalike Audiences**: Finding users similar to existing customers

### Ad Placement
- **Feed Ads**: Appearing in user feeds
- **Story Ads**: Appearing in story format
- **Sidebar Ads**: Appearing on the side of pages
- **Sponsored Content**: Integrated promotional content

## Premium Features Across Platforms

### Cross-Platform Premium
- **Instagram Premium**: Extra monetization and features
- **Twitter Premium**: X Premium for creators and users
- **LinkedIn Premium**: Professional subscription features
- **Netflix and Disney+**: Streaming service subscriptions

### Subscription Content
- **Subscription Model**: How premium works
- **Content Exclusivity**: Exclusive content for paid users
- **User-Specific Content**: Tailored content based on subscription

## Video Streaming Ads

### Streaming Platforms
- **Netflix**: Ad-supported vs. ad-free tiers
- **Disney+**: Advertising integration options
- **YouTube**: Pre-roll and mid-roll ads
- **Streaming Quality**: Video quality for different connection speeds

### Ad Formats
- **Pre-roll Ads**: Ads before video starts
- **Mid-roll Ads**: Ads in the middle of video
- **Post-roll Ads**: Ads after video ends
- **Skippable Ads**: Allowing users to skip after 5 seconds

## Advertiser Dashboard

### Analytics and Reporting
- **Impressions**: Number of times ads are shown
- **Clicks**: Number of ad clicks
- **Conversions**: Completed actions from ads
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**: Measuring advertising effectiveness

### Campaign Management
- **Budget Control**: Setting daily and lifetime budgets
- **Schedule Control**: Choosing when ads run
- **Performance Monitoring**: Real-time campaign metrics
- **A/B Testing**: Testing different ad variations
