# How Meeting Applications Work

## Overview

Video conferencing platforms enable real-time audio and video communication between multiple participants.

## Major Platforms

### Video Conference Services
- **Google Meet**: Google's video conferencing platform
- **Zoom**: Popular third-party meeting platform
- **Microsoft Teams**: Enterprise communication platform

## Core Features

### Screen Sharing
- **Display Streaming**: Sharing your screen or specific applications
- **Resolution Handling**: Optimizing quality for network conditions
- **Permission Control**: Allowing or restricting screen sharing

### Audio System
- **Microphone Detection**: Automatic microphone detection and setup
- **Audio Quality**: Codec selection and compression
- **Mute Functionality**: Muting/unmuting microphone
- **Speaker Management**: Audio output routing

### Video System
- **Camera Integration**: Video camera hardware integration
- **Video Quality**: Resolution and frame rate settings
- **Hardware Acceleration**: Using hardware for video processing
- **Camera Permissions**: Managing camera access

### Participant Management
- **User List**: Viewing all meeting participants
- **Recording**: Recording meetings for later playback
- **Chat**: Text messaging during meetings
- **Virtual Backgrounds**: Background replacement and blur

## Network Requirements

### Bandwidth
- **Minimum Requirements**: Internet speed requirements
- **Quality of Service**: Network optimization for meetings
- **Connection Stability**: Handling network interruptions
